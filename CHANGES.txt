LATEST ADDITION (round 5): Messages and announcements  (page /rep/messages)
  - A school representative can send a one-way message or announcement to the coaches and athletes of their own
    school: all coaches, all athletes, verified athletes only, coaches and athletes, or one person; each can be
    narrowed to one sport. The page shows "This will notify N people" before sending and asks to confirm. An
    empty audience is refused.
  - Who can be reached is exactly who the rep can already see: affiliated coaches with an active account, and the
    athletes on My Athletes (not hidden, not suspended). Pending applicants, removed coaches and other schools are
    never reached. Up to 2,000 people per message (narrow by sport beyond that); 20 messages an hour, 100 a day.
  - Recipients get a bell notification that names the sender, their position and the school, then the message
    (line breaks kept). It is one-way: they cannot reply. The rep keeps a "Sent messages" history (own messages only).

LATEST ADDITIONS (round 3): scholarship edit/delete, CSV exports, Dashboard charts
  - Scholarships (/scholarships, representative view): each scholarship you posted now has Edit and Delete.
    Edit changes the name, sport, coverage, details, requirements and deadline (the school and open/closed state are not
    changed); athletes who applied (and weren't turned down or didn't withdraw) are notified what changed. An expired
    scholarship can still be edited as long as its deadline isn't moved to another past date.
    Delete asks first and is refused once ANY athlete has applied (even if they withdrew): their records are kept, so
    close it instead. Edit and delete are for the poster (or an admin) only, and are audited.
  - "Export CSV" on My Coaches, My Athletes and Coach Requests: downloads every row matching the current search and
    filters (not just the visible page), for your own school only. Athletes' emails, credential numbers and review
    notes are never exported; cells that start with = + - @ are made safe for Excel; every download is audited.
  - Dashboard charts: athletes by sport (verified / not yet verified), coaches by sport, coach requests per month for
    the last six months. They use the same rules as the list pages, so the numbers always agree with them.
  NEW  server/rep-exports.server.ts, server/rep-charts.server.ts, src/lib/repExportApi.ts,
