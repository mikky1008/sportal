## 1. Introduction

Sportal values the privacy and protection of its users' personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sportal collects, uses, stores, processes, and protects personal information when individuals access or use the platform.

Sportal is designed to support student-athletes, coaches, recruiters, educational institutions, and administrators in managing athletic information and discovering sports opportunities.

By using Sportal, users acknowledge that they have been informed about the platform's data processing practices.

## 2. Information We Collect

Sportal may collect the following categories of information depending on the user's role and use of the platform.

### A. Personal Information

- Full name
- Email address
- Contact information, when provided
- Date of birth or age, when required
- Profile photograph
- Account credentials (stored using appropriate security measures)

### B. Academic and Institutional Information

- School or university name
- Academic year or grade level, when applicable
- School affiliation
- Coach or institutional authorization details
- Verification documents, when required

### C. Athletic Information

- Sport and playing position
- Athletic achievements
- Training records
- Skill assessments
- Performance statistics
- Competition records
- Uploaded training and performance videos

### D. Account and Technical Information

Sportal may collect certain technical information, such as:

- Account activity
- Login records
- Device and browser information
- Platform usage information
- Error and system activity logs

The platform should only collect information that is necessary for its intended functions.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Sportal collects and processes information for the following purposes:

- To create and manage user accounts.
- To verify user identities and school affiliations.
- To maintain student-athlete profiles.
- To record and monitor athletic performance.
- To provide performance statistics and AI-assisted assessments.
- To display athlete profiles and leaderboards according to visibility settings.
- To help coaches and recruiters discover potential athletes.
- To manage scholarship listings and applications.
- To facilitate communication between authorized users.
- To maintain platform security and prevent unauthorized access.
- To improve the platform's features and functionality.

## 4. Information Access and Sharing

Sportal applies role-based access to help ensure that users can only access information authorized for their roles.

### A. Student-Athletes

Student-Athletes can manage their own profiles and athletic records according to available platform permissions.

Their profile visibility may depend on the platform's privacy settings.

### B. Coaches and Recruiters

Coaches and recruiters may access athlete information that is made available to them through authorized platform features.

They must not misuse, distribute, or disclose private athlete information without proper authorization.

### C. School Representatives

School Representatives may access institutional information and authorized records related to their respective schools or universities.

They must not access information belonging to other institutions without authorization.

### D. Sportal Administrators

Sportal Administrators may access information necessary for account verification, platform administration, security, and moderation.

Administrative access should only be used for legitimate platform purposes.

### E. Third Parties

Sportal does not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with authorized service providers or other parties when necessary for platform operations, with appropriate safeguards and a lawful basis.

Information may also be disclosed when required or permitted by applicable laws and regulations.

## 5. Athlete Profile Visibility

Sportal may allow athlete profiles and selected performance information to be viewed by other users.

The visibility of information depends on the platform's available privacy settings and permissions.

Users should be aware that information made publicly visible may be viewed by individuals outside their immediate school or institution.

Private documents, verification files, and sensitive account information must not be displayed publicly.

## 6. Videos and Performance Assessments

Student-Athletes may upload videos for performance tracking and AI-assisted assessment.

These videos may be processed to generate athletic feedback, performance scores, and progress summaries.

Sportal should only make these videos accessible to authorized users or audiences selected through applicable visibility settings.

Users must ensure that uploaded videos do not violate another individual's privacy or rights.

AI-generated assessments are intended for informational and developmental purposes. They are not guaranteed to provide completely accurate results.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

Sportal aims to protect personal information through appropriate organizational, technical, and physical security measures.

These may include:

- Secure account authentication.
- Password protection and appropriate password storage.
- Role-based access restrictions.
- Controlled access to sensitive documents.
- Database security measures.
- Monitoring of unauthorized account activity.
- Appropriate backup and recovery procedures.

However, no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

Sportal will take reasonable steps to address security incidents and comply with applicable data breach notification requirements.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Sportal retains personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Users may request the correction or deletion of their personal information through the available platform procedures.

When an account is deleted, associated personal information should be deleted or anonymized when appropriate, unless retention is required or permitted by law.

Certain records may be retained for legitimate administrative, security, or legal purposes.

## 9. Rights of Users

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), users may have the following rights, subject to applicable conditions and exceptions:

- **Right to be Informed:** Know how personal information is collected and processed.
- **Right to Access:** Request access to personal information held by Sportal.
- **Right to Object:** Object to certain forms of personal information processing.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Right to Erasure or Blocking:** Request the deletion or blocking of personal information when legally justified.
- **Right to Data Portability:** Request a copy of personal information in an appropriate format, when applicable.
- **Right to Damages:** Seek compensation for damages resulting from unlawful or unauthorized processing, when legally justified.
- **Right to File a Complaint:** File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission when appropriate.

Users may exercise their rights by contacting the Sportal privacy contact provided below.

## 10. Protection of Minors

Sportal recognizes that some student-athletes may be below 18 years old.

For users who are minors, appropriate parental or legal guardian involvement and consent will be obtained where required by applicable law.

Sportal should apply additional safeguards when handling the personal information, uploaded videos, and documents of minor users.

The platform should also restrict unauthorized access to sensitive information belonging to minors.

## 11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Sportal may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary to support account authentication, maintain user sessions, and improve platform functionality.

Where applicable, users will be informed about the use of these technologies and their available settings.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sportal may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in platform features, data processing practices, or applicable laws.

Users will be notified of significant changes through appropriate platform notifications or other communication methods.

The latest version will be made available through the Sportal platform.
